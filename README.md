## 🚀 What is team-rental-desk?

Team Rental Desk is a desktop application that helps you manage shared rental spaces, seats, payments, and renewals. It uses your local computer to keep all data private and secure, without needing an internet connection. This app is built for Windows users who want a simple yet powerful way to track team rentals.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Local-first storage**: All your data stays on your computer, no cloud needed, so your information is always under your control.
- **Billing tracking**: Easily record and track payments, invoices, and renewals for each rental.
- **Shared space management**: Assign desks, rooms, or seats to team members quickly.
- **ChatGPT integration**: Use AI-powered chat to get insights or help with your rental data.
- **Electron-based**: Runs as a smooth desktop application without needing command-line tools.
- **SQLite database**: Fast and reliable data storage that requires no setup.

## 📋 Getting Started

After installation, start the application from your Start menu or desktop shortcut.

1. **Set up your first space**: Click “Add Space” and enter a name (e.g., “Main Office”).
2. **Manage seats**: Select your space and click “Add Seat” to assign a seat to a team member.
3. **Track payments**: Use the “Billing” section to record a payment amount and date.
4. **Use ChatGPT**: Click the chat icon to ask questions about your data, like “What tenants have overdue payments?”

## 🔧 System Requirements

- **Operating System**: Windows 10, 11 (or newer)
- **Processor**: 1 GHz or faster

- **Storage**: 500 MB available space

## ❓ Troubleshooting

- **App does not start**: Ensure you are running the latest version of Windows and have allowed the installer from your antivirus software.
- **Profile not saving**: Check that your computer has enough storage space and that the app has permission to write data.
- **ChatGPT not working**: Verify your internet connection and that ChatGPT is enabled in settings.
